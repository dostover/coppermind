Tavern Dice

Rules v0.10.1 · best of 3 · 52 dice

Each turn: Roll (active player first), Influence in two steps — Fortune (Reroll, Focus, Mulligan, Distract) then Tactics (abilities, Jam); in each, one action at a time, active player first, two passes end the step — Resolution (everything at once; active player wins ties). ♥ Mend: each ♥ restores 1 Resolve lost this turn — never above where you started the turn. Closing Time: from turn 8 both players lose 1 Resolve each turn; ♥ can't mend it. The bar at the bottom always shows the next step: press its big button (or Space) to roll, pass, allow or start the next turn. A player who can't do anything passes automatically (switch this off under the Showdown). Use abilities with the Use buttons in each player's ability list; Auto abilities apply at Resolution. The Gold actions panel under each player's dice holds the six universal actions; the ones you can use right now light up (you can also tap a die). Actions that target the other player (Distract, Jam, Envenom, Sneaky) are declared in the center first, so they can Ward or Allow. Gold and Resolve update on their own: Gold is paid when you spend it, and 💰 rolled this turn, damage and healing are applied at Resolution — each meter shows what's coming (use Adjust only to fix a mistake). The Showdown in the middle adds up both players' dice side by side — after poison and abilities — shows how much damage each would deal, and what would happen if the turn resolved now.

Rolls use your browser's random number generator. Loadouts and names are remembered on this device only.
