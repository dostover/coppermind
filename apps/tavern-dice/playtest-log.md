# Tavern Dice — Playtest Log

## Match 1 (2026-10-06, rules v0.3, Claude played both sides, real random rolls)
- **Brother Aldric (defense):** Soldier, Guardian, Knight, Cleric, Oracle
- **Vex (Gold/aggro):** Gambler, Dragon, Mage, Assassin, Berserker
- **Result:** Aldric won in round 5. Aldric ended at 16 Resolve (started 10, net healing every round). Vex went 10 → 7 → 5 → 2 → 1 → dead.

Vex's 13 Resolve lost: 6 from Aldric's ⚔, 4 from Vex's own ☠, 3 from Knight's Counterattack.
Vex earned ~14 Gold and spent ~11, nearly all on Rerolls of skulls and Distracts to survive. Aldric earned 2 Gold. Omen (free Reroll) was used every round.

### Findings
- The defensive loadout dominated. Stand Fast + Formation + uncapped ♥ meant Aldric never had a net loss. Knight's Counterattack did damage in 3 of 5 rounds.
- Distract can backfire against heavy-🛡 dice: Distracting Guardian's ⚔ usually turns it into 🛡.
- The Gold engine (Gambler + Dragon) was not runaway: the Gold went into fixing Vex's own skulls rather than pressure.
- Gambler's ☠☠ face plus High Roller was punishing; 4 of Vex's losses were self-inflicted.
- Last Call never came into play (match ended in round 5).

### Rules questions raised
1. **Bloodlust timing:** with simultaneous resolution, does "lost Resolve this round" include this round's ☠ and incoming ⚔? As played: no, so it never triggered.
2. **Backstab:** +1 per ⚔ symbol on Assassin's die, or +1 total?
3. **Knight's Counterattack with pooled 🛡:** played as "triggers if any ⚔ is blocked while Knight shows 🛡." Needs an official ruling.
4. **Stand Fast vs. Arcane Surge:** played as Stand Fast reducing unblockable damage (it's a Resolve-loss reduction, not a block).
