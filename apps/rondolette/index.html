      How to play
      • All six balls start in the middle of the bowl.
      • Press where you want to set the top down, drag in the direction you want it to travel, and let go. A longer drag spins it faster.
      • The spinning top wanders, knocks the balls, and flings them round the bowl. Each ball that settles in a rim hollow or rolls into a corner box scores that number.
      • The red ball scores double. The blue ball's points are taken away.
      • Score with all six balls in one spin and you get another turn.
      • Balls that jump the rim are out for that spin.
      • First to 1,000: players take turns and add up their points. The first to reach the target wins.
      • Online: create a table, send the invite link, and everyone takes a seat from their own device. On your turn you flick the top; everyone else watches the same spin live.
      • Single spin-off: everyone gets one spin and the highest spin wins. Players who tie for the top spin go again until one is ahead.
