Pixel Party Dungeon

Build your party, then dive into the glowing caves. Platform in real time, fight in turns, and roll the big dice. One screen, 1–4 players on keyboard or controllers.

Claude writes a new villain for each adventure. This prototype has 3 levels; the last one ends with a lieutenant fight.
