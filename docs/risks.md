# Risk & Action Item Tracker

Based on the Product Team Coach risk-grid exercise (Inception session, Chapter 5.6).

A risk is something *unrelated to the product itself* that could disrupt the
team, the business, or the users if it goes unaddressed (e.g. high turnover,
a hiring freeze, a budget cut) — not a reason to avoid starting work, just
something to monitor. Every risk that makes the cut gets a named owner and
stays on this list, "brought up daily" (or on whatever cadence the team
actually meets), until it's resolved.

Add a row whenever a risk-grid exercise surfaces something worth tracking —
typically during an Inception session, but any time is fine.

## Open risks

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Owner | Status | Date raised | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|-------|--------|-------------|-------|
| _(example) Key vendor could deprecate the API we depend on_ | High | Less likely | _unassigned_ | Open | YYYY-MM-DD | _(remove this example row once real risks are added)_ |

**Impact:** High / Low — would this meaningfully hurt the product, team, or business if it happened?
**Likelihood:** More likely / Less likely — how probable is it, realistically?

Focus follow-up energy on the High Impact + More Likely quadrant first — that's
where the book says most of the real danger clusters.

## How to use this

1. During a risk-grid exercise, add every risk that made someone's top-3 list, with its impact/likelihood placement.
2. Assign an owner to each one right away — an unowned risk doesn't get chased.
3. Bring open risks up at whatever cadence the team's ceremonies run on (see the `run-product-ceremony` skill) until each is resolved.
4. Move resolved items to the Resolved table with a short note on how it was addressed, rather than deleting them — the history is useful later.
