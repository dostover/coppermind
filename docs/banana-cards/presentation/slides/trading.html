Trading

Social, not financial, by design

In person only

Both fans scan and both confirm. It works anywhere two fans meet, not only at the ballpark.

No marketplace grammar

No prices, no rarity counts, no browsable list of strangers’ cards.

Sets give the reason

“Finishes your set” on a friend’s card tells you what to ask for.

A digital trade screen drifts toward looking like a shop. That’s the product’s biggest risk, so every trade screen is designed against it.

Banana Cards · 7

Choosing a single-use code over a physical card moved all the weight of “social, not financial” onto the trade screen. Two hard constraints follow. First, trades require two fans to be physically together: a mutual QR scan, the same gesture as redeeming. Together, not necessarily at the ballpark, which matters for fans without tickets. Second, no marketplace grammar: if the UI reads like a shop, the premise dies in the UI whatever is true underneath. Sets supply a reason to trade that’s about the collection, never about value.
