Five sources of value

What makes a card worth having

1

Presence

You had to be there. Lives in keepsakes.

2

Story

Characters and bits, more than stats: the tux, the dancing umpire.

3

Trading

The social ritual of the swap, in person, never a price.

4

Completion

Themed sets: the payoff is finishing the story.

5

Access

Small, real perks: a shoutout, a clip, a photo op.

None of the five depends on a card being rare. All five are built into the demo.

Banana Cards · 4

These five replace scarcity as the reason a card matters. Presence is first and is the one the design protects most carefully; it lives in keepsakes, which we’ll see in a minute. Sets and perks are the answer to the between-tours problem: most fans see a team about once a year, and these give the collection a reason to be opened in between.
