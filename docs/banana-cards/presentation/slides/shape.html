How it works

A cheap code out there, a rich card on your phone

1 · Get a code

In the program, on a seat, from a player, on the stream, in a post

2 · Scan it

Any phone camera. No app; a new fan joins with just a name

3 · Tap to reveal

The card waits face down. Flip it to see what you got

4 · Collect

Set progress right away; finished sets unlock perks

One gesture for everything. Redeeming, trading and passing a card on all start with a scan.

Breakeven by design. The physical side is a cheap, single-use code; the rich card costs nothing to copy.

Banana Cards · 5

Getting the card is the core experience: every fan does it first, and plenty will never trade. So it’s fast: under a minute, before first pitch or between innings, then the phone goes back in the pocket. The app never needs attention during a bit, and codes stay claimable after the game. The code was originally imagined as a token handed out at the gate, but it can live anywhere: a program, a seat, a player’s hat, a stream overlay.
