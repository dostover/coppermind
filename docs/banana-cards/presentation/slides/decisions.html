Banana Cards · 11

Each row is a place where the obvious answer would have undercut the thesis. A printed card fails breakeven across six teams and leaves remote fans out, so the physical touch became a cheap code, and positioning has to address the NFT association head-on. Presence moved into keepsakes so trading can’t dilute it. The kids decision came from noticing that quick join, which asks only for a name, conflicted with COPPA; rather than build parental consent now, accounts are 13+ with a neutral year-of-birth question, and families share a grown-up’s account, which also means a parent is part of every kid’s trade. Finally, I stopped trying to prevent a market: anything tradeable can be priced somewhere, so the goal is that the product never manufactures scarcity. Not legal advice; the age screen needs a lawyer’s review before real fans.
