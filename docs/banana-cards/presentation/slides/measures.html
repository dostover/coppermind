How we’d know it’s working

Better nights, and more fans

Fan base growth

  • New fans per Pass It On card
  • Stream and drop claims that become collectors
  • First-timers who arrive holding cards

Fan experience

  • Share of fans at a game who claim a card
  • Collectors who trade at least once
  • Collectors who return between tours

Signals of harm

  • App use during the show (should be near zero)
  • Real resale prices: scarcity crept in
  • Complaints from families or first-timers

Caution: while an account costs only a name, one person can inflate growth counts. The metrics need that guard before they mean much.

Banana Cards · 14

Banana Ball’s north star is fan experience, so Banana Cards is judged on two things together: does it make the night better, and does it grow the fan base. Exact metrics are still to be picked; these are candidates. The harm signals matter as much as the growth ones. Real resale prices aren’t something to police; they tell us where the design let scarcity in.
