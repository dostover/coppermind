A product thought experiment

Banana Cards

Trading cards the Banana Ball way: value from being there, not from scarcity.

Concept to working demo · Not affiliated with the Savannah Bananas, Fans First Entertainment or the Banana Ball Championship League

Open with the one-line idea: Banana Ball rewrote baseball around fans; Banana Cards does the same for trading cards. This is a thought experiment, built end to end: the opportunity, the value model, the decisions and tradeoffs, and a working demo. Say up front that it isn’t affiliated with the team or league.
