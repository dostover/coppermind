Every channel gives pool cards to trade and keepsakes of their own. Remote fans trade face to face too: at watch parties, at home, anywhere they meet.

Banana Cards · 8

Banana Ball tickets are very hard to get. If the best cards required attendance, the product would reward the lucky few, the opposite of Fans First. Streamed games are a big part of how the fan base grows, so streams carry both kinds of card. Stream cards are live-only: “you were watching live” is the online version of “you had to be there.” Social drops have a plain end date and no countdowns, no “only 100 left,” no follow-to-win. Every card is honest about where it came from, and that label stays with it after a trade.
